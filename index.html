About ITI

Government ITI Simri Bakhtiyarpur provides Industrial Training and skill-oriented technical education to students. The institute focuses on practical training, employability and development of industry-relevant technical skills.

Principal's Message

Welcome to Government ITI Simri Bakhtiyarpur

Our aim is to provide quality skill training, practical learning and better career opportunities to students. Students are encouraged to develop technical skills, discipline and professional values.
